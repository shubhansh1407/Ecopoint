Verifiable Process

The 4-Stage Custody Chain

Points are never awarded on self-reporting alone. Traceability ensures zero fraud and clean recycling streams.

01 Citizen

Reusable Bag Deposit

Citizen segregates waste at home into tagged smart reusable bags, submits estimated weight, and requests municipal pickup.

02 Collector

Custody Handover

Municipal route collector scans bag code, confirms physical custody handover at the doorstep, and routes cargo to recovery facility.

03 Facility

Weighing & Verification

Facility operators weigh bag on industrial scales, verify material category, and assign circular recovery outcome.

04 EcoWallet

Verified Rewards

Net points are credited to citizen's EcoWallet and logged into the tamper-evident ledger. Redeemable for transit & community vouchers.
